# Diaspora Mode — Revenue Model Strategy

Options to monetize the Diaspora Mode command center while keeping core trust and accessibility.

---

## 1. Marketplace (TrueRate Direct)

### Transaction-based

- **Platform fee per order**
  - Percentage of order value (e.g. 2–5%) and/or fixed fee (e.g. $1–2).
  - Collected at checkout (Stripe); payout to vendors via Stripe Connect or manual settlement.
- **Delivery fee**
  - Pass-through to logistics partner or margin on delivery fee.
- **Premium placement**
  - Vendors pay for “featured” or “top of category” placement (fixed monthly or per-impression).

**Rationale:** Aligns revenue with value (each diaspora purchase); low friction if fee is transparent and competitive.

---

## 2. Remittance Optimization

- **Affiliate / referral**
  - Partner with remittance providers (e.g. Wave, Sendwave, Wise, local providers). Earn commission when users send via tracked links.
- **Lead gen**
  - “Compare providers” captures intent; sell qualified leads to providers (fixed per lead or CPA).
- **Premium timing alerts**
  - Free: basic “rate favorable today” on site. Premium (subscription or one-off): SMS/push alerts when rate crosses a user-set threshold.

**Rationale:** Diaspora already sends money home; TrueRate adds trust and timing — monetize the referral or the alert.

---

## 3. Investment Navigator

- **Listing fee**
  - Verified projects (real estate, agriculture, SME) pay to be listed and featured.
- **Success fee**
  - If TrueRate facilitates intro or capital flow, take a small % of invested amount or flat success fee.
- **Premium tier**
  - Free: ROI calculator, sector risk, public listings. Premium: early access to deals, detailed project memos, capital pooling access (when built).

**Rationale:** Investors pay for access and trust; project owners pay for distribution.

---

## 4. Trust & Transparency Layer

- **Vendor verification**
  - One-time or annual fee for “TrueRate Verified” (covers due diligence, badge, listing boost).
- **Dispute resolution**
  - Free first dispute; optional premium support or paid escalation for high-value orders.

**Rationale:** Vendors and users pay for trust signals and peace of mind.

---

## 5. Subscription (Diaspora Premium)

- **Monthly / annual**
  - Single “Diaspora Mode Premium” tier: lower marketplace fee, remittance alerts, investment project access, priority support, activity/audit export.
- **Freemium**
  - Free: live rates, compare providers, ROI calculator, browse marketplace. Premium: checkout, alerts, verified projects, dispute support.

**Rationale:** Recurring revenue; positions TrueRate as the default “diaspora command center.”

---

## 6. Recommended Phasing

| Phase | Revenue focus |
|-------|----------------|
| **MVP** | No revenue; focus on traffic and signups to marketplace waitlist or newsletter. |
| **Phase 2** | Marketplace: platform fee (e.g. 3%) + optional delivery margin. Optional vendor verification fee. |
| **Phase 3** | Remittance: affiliate/CPA; Investment: listing fee for verified projects. |
| **Phase 4** | Subscription (Diaspora Premium); premium placement for vendors; optional success fee on investments. |

---

## 7. Metrics to Track

- **Marketplace:** GMV, take rate (%), orders per user, repeat purchase rate, vendor LTV.
- **Remittance:** Clicks to provider, conversion to send, affiliate revenue per user.
- **Investment:** Listing count, project views, “express interest” or contact rate; later, capital deployed.
- **Trust:** Verification fee revenue, dispute volume and resolution rate, NPS or satisfaction post-order.
- **Subscription:** MRR, churn, conversion from free to premium.

Keep fees transparent (e.g. “3% platform fee” at checkout) and messaging mission-aligned (“Supporting Liberian businesses and families”) to preserve trust while scaling revenue.
