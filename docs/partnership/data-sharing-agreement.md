# Data Sharing Agreement (DSA) — Outline & Key Clauses  
## TrueRate Liberia ↔ Ministry of Commerce and Industry (MoCI)

**Document type:** Agreement outline (for legal review)  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Template — to be finalized with MoCI and legal counsel

---

## 1. Parties

- **Data Provider:** Ministry of Commerce and Industry (MoCI), Republic of Liberia.  
- **Data Recipient:** TrueRate Liberia (“Recipient”).

---

## 2. Definitions

- **Agreement:** This Data Sharing Agreement.  
- **MoU:** Memorandum of Understanding between the Parties.  
- **Data:** Datasets made available by MoCI under this Agreement (e.g. commodity prices, trade statistics, licensing status, aggregated complaints).  
- **Permitted Purpose:** Fintech analytics, market verification, and public market transparency as described in the MoU and Section 3.  
- **Personal Data:** Data relating to an identified or identifiable natural person. The Parties intend that Data shared hereunder be **non-personal** or **aggregated/anonymized** where applicable.

---

## 3. Scope and Permitted Use

- **Datasets:** As specified in an Annex (e.g. commodity price monitoring data, trade/import statistics, business licensing status, aggregated consumer complaint indicators).  
- **Permitted use:** Recipient may use the Data only for the Permitted Purpose: operating the TrueRate platform, providing market intelligence and verification features, and producing reports for MoCI as agreed.  
- **Restrictions:** Recipient shall not sell, sublicense, or commercially exploit the Data for purposes outside the Permitted Purpose. Recipient shall not attempt to re-identify any anonymized or aggregated data.

---

## 4. Delivery and Format

- **Method:** As agreed (API, secure file transfer, or other means specified by MoCI).  
- **Format:** JSON, CSV, Excel, or other format specified in the Annex.  
- **Frequency:** As per Annex (e.g. daily, weekly, monthly).  
- **Changes:** MoCI may change format or frequency with reasonable notice; Recipient will adapt systems within an agreed timeframe.

---

## 5. Security and Confidentiality

- Recipient shall store and process Data using appropriate technical and organizational measures (access control, encryption where applicable, audit logging).  
- Recipient shall limit access to Data to personnel and systems that need it for the Permitted Purpose.  
- Recipient shall not disclose Data to third parties except as required by law or with MoCI’s prior written consent.  
- If Personal Data is inadvertently shared, Recipient shall handle it in accordance with applicable data protection law and MoCI instructions.

---

## 6. Retention and Deletion

- Recipient shall retain Data only for the period necessary for the Permitted Purpose and as specified in the Annex.  
- Upon termination or expiry, Recipient shall delete or return Data as instructed by MoCI, and certify completion if requested.

---

## 7. Attribution and Public Use

- Where Data is displayed to the public, Recipient shall attribute it to the Ministry of Commerce and Industry (or as MoCI specifies).  
- Recipient may describe the partnership in marketing and reports in a manner agreed with MoCI.

---

## 8. Compliance and Audit

- Recipient shall comply with applicable Liberian law and with MoCI policies communicated in writing.  
- Recipient shall maintain records of access, use, and security measures related to the Data.  
- MoCI (or its designee) may request a compliance report or audit on reasonable notice; Recipient shall cooperate and provide information necessary to verify compliance.

---

## 9. Liability and Indemnity

- [To be drafted with legal counsel: limitation of liability, indemnity for breach, and allocation of risk.]

---

## 10. Term and Termination

- **Term:** As specified (e.g. initial term of two (2) years, renewable by agreement).  
- **Termination:** Either party may terminate on written notice as specified (e.g. 90 days). MoCI may terminate immediately for material breach.  
- **Effect of termination:** Sections on confidentiality, retention/deletion, and liability shall survive. Recipient shall cease use and delete/return Data as in Section 6.

---

## 11. Amendments

- Amendments shall be in writing and signed by both Parties.
