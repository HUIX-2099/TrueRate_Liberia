## 1. Executive Summary

TrueRate Liberia is a fintech and market transparency platform serving Liberian citizens and businesses with reliable exchange rate information, market intelligence, and financial tools. We are requesting a **formal data-sharing partnership** with the Ministry of Commerce and Industry (MoCI) to integrate official commerce data into our platform. This collaboration will:

- **Strengthen market transparency** by surfacing official commodity prices and trade statistics to the public.
- **Support fintech analytics** by linking commerce data to exchange rates and cost-of-living indicators.
- **Enhance institutional credibility** for both MoCI and TrueRate through verified, attributable government data.
- **Enable future expansion** toward SME insights, investor dashboards, and Central Bank collaboration.

We propose a structured partnership governed by a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) and a Data Sharing Agreement (DSA), with clear purposes, usage limits, and compliance frameworks.

---

## 2. About TrueRate Liberia

- **Mission:** Provide accurate, accessible exchange rate and market information to reduce information asymmetry and support financial decisions in Liberia.
- **Current offerings:** Live and historical LRD/USD rates, rate predictions, regional breakdowns, converter, market intelligence dashboards, commodity price monitoring, trade analytics, and community tools (forums, fraud reporting).
- **Technical capacity:** Next.js application, dedicated ingestion service for external data, PostgreSQL and Redis infrastructure, audit logging, and API-based integration patterns.
- **Audience:** General public, SMEs, money changers, remittance users, and (with expanded data) policymakers and investors.

## 3. Proposed Collaboration Scope

### 3.1 Data We Request Access To

| Priority | Data source | Purpose |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| 1 | **Commodity price monitoring data** | Display official prices; power cost-of-living and inflation-related analytics. |
| 2 | **Trade / import statistics** | Market demand indicators, trade trend analysis, and transparency reporting. |
| 3 | **Business registration / licensing status** (if available) | Business verification and “verified changer” legitimacy checks. |
| 4 | **Consumer complaint records** (aggregated, anonymized) | Risk and fraud indicators; no personal data required. |

We are prepared to accept data in the format MoCI can provide: **API (preferred), CSV/Excel exports, or scheduled reports**, and will adapt our ingestion pipeline accordingly.

### 3.2 How TrueRate Will Use the Data

- **Fintech analytics:** Link commodity prices to FX and cost-of-living views.
- **Verification:** Support business legitimacy and license-status checks where applicable.
- **Market transparency:** Publish attributable, official commodity and trade insights to the public.
- **No resale or repurposing:** Data will be used only for the purposes agreed in the MoU/DSA and will not be sold or licensed to third parties for commercial use outside the agreed scope.

### 3.3 What We Offer MoCI

- **Attribution and visibility:** Clear “Ministry of Commerce” or “Official source” labeling wherever data is displayed.
- **Public good:** Broader reach of official data to citizens and businesses.
- **Technical integration:** We handle normalization, storage, and secure access; MoCI retains full ownership and control of data.
- **Reporting:** Periodic summary reports on usage and impact, as agreed in the MoU.

## 4. Proposed Governance

- **MoU:** High-level partnership objectives, roles, and commitment to a Data Sharing Agreement.
- **Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):** Specific datasets, purposes, formats, frequency, security, retention, and compliance.
- **Data governance policy:** TrueRate’s internal policy for handling ministry data (access control, audit, breach response).
- **Review points:** Annual or bi-annual review of scope and compliance.
