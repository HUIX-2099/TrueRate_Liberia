# University Research Project Brief  
## Liberian Economy Data Verification & Market Intelligence

**Document type:** Project brief for university partners  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Prepared by:** TrueRate Liberia  
**Purpose:** To define scope, deliverables, and attribution for student research on Liberian economy data verification in support of TrueRate and CBL market intelligence.

---

## 1. Project title

**Liberian Economy Data Verification & Market Intelligence**  
*Comparing official (government) data with real-time and alternative sources to improve market transparency and support CBL partnership.*

---

## 2. Objectives

- **Educational:** Give students practical experience in gathering, analyzing, and verifying economic data for Liberia across market risk, price stability, cost of living, commodity prices, and import trends.
- **Analytical:** Produce comparable assessments of government-published data vs. real-time or alternative data (gaps, lags, coverage, consistency).
- **Institutional:** Support TrueRate’s goal of becoming a **market intelligence partner** with the Central Bank of Liberia (CBL) by providing verified, well-documented analysis that can inform policy and public dashboards.
- **Capacity-building:** Help the University foster **real-time finance data collection** and evidence-based economic analysis in Liberia.

---

## 3. Research focus areas

| Area | Official sources (examples) | Real-time / alternative (examples) | Student tasks |
|------|----------------------------|-------------------------------------|----------------|
| **Market risk** | CBL FX bulletins, official rates | TrueRate rates, market surveys, spread indicators | Compare levels and volatility; document timing and coverage. |
| **Price stability** | LISGIS/CBL CPI, inflation releases | Trading Economics, World Bank WDI, TrueRate inflation views | Cross-check series; note revisions and lags. |
| **Cost of living & affordability** | MoCI commodity bulletins, official prices | Market prices, field surveys (if available), TrueRate commodity data | Verify official prices against observed prices; assess basket affordability. |
| **Commodity price trends** | MoCI, CBL, LISGIS | World Bank Pink Sheet, UN Comtrade, TrueRate commodity series | Trend analysis; compare official vs. alternative series. |
| **Import volume trends** | MoCI/LRA trade statistics, customs data | UN Comtrade, port/proxy indicators | Compare official import data with external trade data; assess lags and coverage. |

*Sources are indicative; the University and TrueRate may agree to add or remove sources based on data availability and curriculum.*

---

## 4. Deliverables (suggested)

- **Written report(s):** Short reports or a semester-end report summarizing methodology, data sources, comparison results, and recommendations (e.g. where official and real-time data align or diverge).
- **Structured outputs:** Where feasible, tables, charts, or small dashboards that clearly show “official vs. real-time” (or alternative) for at least two of the five focus areas.
- **Attribution and citations:** All sources (government, TrueRate, third-party) clearly cited; University and participating class/group named as research partner.

Deliverables can be adapted (e.g. single consolidated report, or a series of topic notes) in agreement with the supervising faculty.

---

## 5. Data and support from TrueRate

- **Platform and methodology:** TrueRate can share its approach to exchange rates, commodity prices, and trade data (e.g. data flow, sources, and schema overview) for use in student analysis.
- **Anonymized or aggregated data:** Where appropriate, TrueRate may provide access to aggregated or non-sensitive platform data (e.g. rate series, commodity series) under a simple data-use agreement to support verification exercises.
- **Guest session (optional):** A workshop or guest lecture on data sources, verification methods, and the role of market intelligence in Liberia.

TrueRate will **not** require ownership of student work; the University and students retain academic ownership. TrueRate requests the right to **cite and attribute** the research (with consent) in its materials and in any submissions to the CBL or other partners.

---

## 6. Credit and attribution

- **University and class/group:** The participating class or student group will be **formally credited** in TrueRate’s materials (e.g. “Research supported by [University name], [Class/Group name], [Semester/Year]”) in reports, platform footnotes, or CBL-facing documents where the research is used.
- **Students:** Individual students may be named in project reports or appendices if the University and students agree.
- **Publication:** If the University or students wish to publish the work (e.g. working paper, blog), TrueRate requests acknowledgment as project partner and data/methodology support; no restriction on academic publication.

---

Timeline can be aligned to the University’s academic calendar (e.g. one semester or two).

---

## 8. Legal and ethics

- **Data use:** Any data shared by TrueRate will be used only for this research project and in line with the agreed data-use terms.
- **Attribution:** All third-party sources (CBL, LISGIS, MoCI, UN, World Bank, etc.) will be cited in line with academic standards and TrueRate’s attribution policy.
- **Confidentiality:** No confidential or personally identifiable data will be requested from students; analysis should rely on public or agreed aggregated data only.
