---

## Purpose of the collaboration

We propose a **student-led research initiative** in which a class or designated student group would:

1. **Gather and analyze** data relevant to the Liberian economy from both official (government) sources and real-time or alternative sources.
2. **Compare and verify** government-published data against real-world, real-time observations (e.g. market rates, commodity prices, trade flows).
3. **Produce structured outputs** (reports, dashboards, or datasets) that support market transparency and inform TrueRate’s role as a potential **market intelligence partner** with the Central Bank of Liberia (CBL).

This exercise will deepen students’ understanding of **market risk**, **price stability**, **cost of living and affordability**, **commodity price trends**, and **import volume trends**—core dimensions of the Liberian economy—while contributing to a more accurate and transparent information environment for the country.

---

## Research focus areas

| Focus area | Description | Student role |
|------------|-------------|--------------|
| **Market risk** | FX volatility, liquidity, and risk indicators | Compare CBL/official rates with market rates; document gaps and timing. |
| **Price stability** | Inflation, CPI, and price indices | Cross-check LISGIS/CBL and other official inflation data with real-time price observations. |
| **Cost of living & affordability** | Staple commodity prices, basket costs | Verify official commodity price bulletins against field or market data where feasible. |
| **Commodity price trends** | Rice, palm oil, cement, and other key commodities | Track trends; compare official (e.g. MoCI) series with alternative sources (e.g. markets, surveys). |
| **Import volume trends** | Trade and import statistics | Compare official trade/import data with available real-time or proxy indicators; note lags and coverage. |

Students would be guided to use appropriate methods (e.g. descriptive statistics, time-series comparison, gap analysis) and to cite all sources. TrueRate can provide access to its platform data (exchange rates, commodity and trade schemas, and methodology) to support the analysis, subject to a simple data-use agreement.

---

## What we offer the University and students

- **Full academic credit and attribution:** The participating class or student group will be **formally credited** in TrueRate materials (reports, platform, and any CBL-facing deliverables) for their research and verification work.
- **Real-world relevance:** Students work with live economic issues and real data pipelines, strengthening employability and research skills.
- **Support for real-time finance and data:** The project aligns with the goal of fostering **real-time finance data collection** in Liberia and positions the University as a contributor to evidence-based policy and market transparency.
- **Structured scope:** We can provide a **project brief** (objectives, deliverables, timeline, and data scope) and, if useful, guest lectures or workshops on data sources, verification methods, and TrueRate’s role in the ecosystem.
- **Pathway to policy impact:** High-quality outputs may be shared (with the University’s and students’ consent) with the Central Bank of Liberia and other institutions as TrueRate pursues its goal of becoming a **market intelligence partner** with the CBL.
