## 2. Data Requested

We request access to the following data, in whatever format the Ministry can practicably provide (API, CSV, Excel, or scheduled reports):

| # | Dataset | Description | Preferred format | Frequency |
|---|---------|-------------|------------------|-----------|
| 1 | Commodity price monitoring data | Official commodity prices (e.g. rice, palm oil, cement) and effective dates | JSON/CSV/Excel | Daily or as published |
| 2 | Trade / import statistics | Aggregated or declaration-level import data (ports, commodities, volumes, values) | JSON/CSV/Excel | Weekly or monthly |
| 3 | Business licensing / registration status | Status of licensed businesses (e.g. money changers) for verification only | API or bulk export | As updated |
| 4 | Consumer complaint records | Aggregated, anonymized complaint indicators only; no personal data | Aggregated report | Periodic |

We will accept partial access (e.g. commodity and trade data only) and can phase additional datasets after a successful pilot.

---

## 3. Purpose of Use

- **Fintech analytics:** Linking official commodity prices to exchange rates and cost-of-living indicators on the TrueRate platform.  
- **Verification:** Supporting business legitimacy and license-status checks where data is available.  
- **Market transparency:** Publishing official, attributable commodity and trade insights to the public.  
- **No commercial resale:** We will not sell or sublicense the data to third parties; use is limited to the TrueRate platform and agreed reports to the Ministry.

---

## 4. Legal and Compliance Commitment

- We will comply with applicable Liberian law and any conditions set by the Ministry.  
- We will sign a **Data Sharing Agreement** and **Memorandum of Understanding** defining permitted use, security, retention, and audit.  
- We will adhere to our **Data Governance Policy** and provide compliance documentation as required.

---

## 5. Security and Handling

- Data will be stored in secure, access-controlled systems (PostgreSQL, restricted access; credentials in secrets management).  
- Access will be limited to authorized personnel and systems; audit logging will be maintained.  
- We will not retain data beyond the period agreed in the DSA and will handle any incidents in line with the agreement.
